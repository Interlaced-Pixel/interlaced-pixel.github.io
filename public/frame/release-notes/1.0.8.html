Frame 1.0.8

This update fixes a race that could reset episode watched status after restarting Frame.

  • Preserve the latest watched status and playback progress when a library scan finishes after playback state changes.
  • Keep the library's saved episode status consistent with the live catalog after a scan.

    • Frame is signed with its persistent self-signed certificate and is not Apple notarized. macOS may require manual first-launch approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
